# Free Accessibility Tools Directory

Free screen readers, magnifiers, dictation, captions, reading aids, and ADHD / low-energy helpers — grouped by need and device, each with cost, platform, setup steps, and an official link. No affiliate links. Every link points to an official product or support page.

Published by [Asdale Dynamics, LLC](https://dylanasdale.github.io) (Indiana, formed Oct 2026) — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools.

## How to use this directory

- Browse by **need** (Vision, Hearing, Reading & writing, Motor & dexterity, Focus & energy) or by **device** (Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, Android, Web, ChromeOS).
- Each tool lists cost, platforms, setup steps, and an official link.
- **"Built in"** means the feature comes free with the operating system or browser — nothing to download.
- The companion site (`index.html`) is a static HTML page with client-side search and filters; it runs entirely in your browser with no install or build step.

## By need

### Vision — Screen readers

#### NVDA
- **Cost:** Free (open source; donations welcome)
- **Platforms:** Windows
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Download the installer from nvaccess.org, run it, and follow the first-time setup (choose keyboard layout and speech preferences). Press Insert+N to open the NVDA menu anytime.
- **Official link:** https://www.nvaccess.org/

#### VoiceOver (macOS)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver and turn it on. Press Command+F5 to toggle. The first launch offers a VoiceOver tutorial to learn the cursor and commands.
- **Official link:** https://www.apple.com/accessibility/mac/vision/

#### VoiceOver (iOS)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver and turn it on. Use the Accessibility Shortcut (triple-click the side or Home button) to toggle it quickly.
- **Official link:** https://www.apple.com/accessibility/iphone/vision/

#### Narrator
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Windows at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Press Windows+Ctrl+Enter to turn Narrator on or off. Open Settings > Accessibility > Narrator to change voices, speed, and verbosity.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/windows/complete-guide-to-narrator

#### TalkBack
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Android
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Android at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack and turn it on. Hold both volume keys for 3 seconds to toggle TalkBack (enable the shortcut first).
- **Official link:** https://support.google.com/android/answer/6283877

#### Orca
- **Cost:** Free (open source)
- **Platforms:** Linux
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Preinstalled on many GNOME-based distributions. Enable it in Settings > Accessibility > Screen Reader, or install the 'orca' package with your distro's package manager.
- **Official link:** https://wiki.gnome.org/Projects/Orca

#### ChromeVox
- **Cost:** Free (open source)
- **Platforms:** ChromeOS, Chrome (any OS)
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** On Chromebooks, press Ctrl+Alt+Z to toggle ChromeVox. On other computers, add the ChromeVox extension from the Chrome Web Store.
- **Official link:** https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/chromevox/kgeglglhpdcpeamgmbcinpolncjlcdje

### Vision — Screen magnifiers

#### Windows Magnifier
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Windows at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Press Windows+Plus (+) to zoom in and Windows+Minus (-) to zoom out. Press Windows+Esc to close. Choose full-screen, docked, or lens view in Settings > Accessibility > Magnifier.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/windows/use-magnifier-to-make-things-on-the-screen-easier-to-see

#### macOS Zoom
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > Zoom and enable 'Use keyboard shortcuts to zoom' or 'Use scroll gesture with modifier keys to zoom'. Toggle with Option+Command+8.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-zoom-on-mac-mh25603/mac

#### iOS Zoom
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Zoom and turn it on. Double-tap with three fingers to zoom, drag three fingers to move around the screen.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/zoom-magnify-the-screen-iphone

#### Android Magnification
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Android
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Android at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Magnification and choose a shortcut (triple-tap screen or accessibility button). Tap the shortcut to magnify the part of the screen you touch.
- **Official link:** https://support.google.com/android/answer/9206290

#### KMag
- **Cost:** Free (open source)
- **Platforms:** Linux
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Install the 'kmag' package using your distribution's package manager (part of KDE Gear), then launch KMag from your applications menu.
- **Official link:** https://apps.kde.org/kmag/

### Hearing — Captions / transcription

#### Windows Live Captions
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows (Windows 11)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Windows 11 at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Press Windows+Ctrl+L to turn Live Captions on. Download the language pack when prompted, then choose your caption style and microphone source in Settings > Accessibility > Captions.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/windows/use-live-captions-to-understand-audio-better

#### macOS Live Captions
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > Live Captions (beta) and turn it on. Captions appear in a floating window for any audio playing on your Mac.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-live-captions-on-mac-mh42238/mac

#### Chrome Live Caption
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Chrome (any OS)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into the Chrome browser at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Chrome Settings > Accessibility and turn on Live Caption. Captions appear automatically for videos and audio playing in Chrome tabs.
- **Official link:** https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/99619157

#### Live Transcribe & Sound Notifications
- **Cost:** Free (app from Google)
- **Platforms:** Android
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Install Live Transcribe from the Play Store, then enable it in Settings > Accessibility > Live Transcribe. It transcribes speech in real time and can alert you to sounds like doorbells or alarms.
- **Official link:** https://support.google.com/android/answer/9206238

#### iOS Live Captions
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS 16 and later at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Live Captions (beta) and turn it on. Type-to-speak responses are also supported during FaceTime and phone calls.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-live-captions-iphone

### Motor & dexterity — Voice control / dictation

#### Windows Voice Access
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows (Windows 11)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Windows 11 at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Speech > Voice Access and turn it on. Download the language pack when prompted, then say 'Voice Access wake up' to begin controlling your PC by voice.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/windows/use-voice-access-to-control-your-pc-with-your-voice

#### Windows Dictation
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Windows at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Press Windows+H in any text field to start dictation. Say punctuation like 'period' or 'comma' as you speak. Enable online speech recognition in Settings > Privacy & security > Speech.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/windows/dictate-text-using-speech-recognition

#### macOS Dictation
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Keyboard > Dictation and turn it on. Press the Fn key (or the Dictation key) twice to start speaking wherever you can type.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/dictate-text-on-mac

#### iOS Dictation
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > General > Keyboard and turn on Enable Dictation. Tap the microphone icon on the keyboard to dictate text in any app.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/dictate-text-on-iphone

#### macOS Voice Control
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > Voice Control and turn it on. Number overlays and a grid let you click, scroll, and navigate entirely by voice.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/control-your-mac-using-voice-control

#### iOS Voice Control
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Voice Control and turn it on. Use numbered item overlays or a screen grid to tap, swipe, and type by voice.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/control-iphone-with-voice-control

#### Gboard Voice Typing
- **Cost:** Free (keyboard app from Google)
- **Platforms:** Android, iOS
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Install Gboard from the Play Store (Android) or App Store (iOS), set it as your keyboard, then tap the microphone icon on the keyboard to dictate text.
- **Official link:** https://gboard.blogspot.com/

### Motor & dexterity — Switch / adaptive input

#### macOS Switch Control
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > Switch Control and turn it on. Connect one or more adaptive switches and use scanning to navigate and control your Mac.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-switch-control-on-mac

#### iOS Switch Control
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Switch Control and turn it on. Pair adaptive switches or use the screen as a trigger to scan and select items.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/use-switch-control-on-iphone

### Reading & writing — Reading aid

#### Microsoft Immersive Reader
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Web, iOS, Android
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Edge, OneNote, Word, and Outlook at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open a web page in Microsoft Edge and press F9 (or select the Immersive Reader icon in the address bar). Use it to read aloud, change text size, spacing, fonts, and highlight parts of speech.
- **Official link:** https://support.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/use-immersive-reader-in-microsoft-edge

#### macOS Spoken Content
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** macOS
- **Built in:** Yes — built into macOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content. Enable 'Speak selection' and choose a keyboard shortcut, or turn on 'Speak screen' to have items read aloud.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/use-spoken-content-on-mac

#### iOS Spoken Content
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** iOS (iPhone, iPad)
- **Built in:** Yes — built into iOS at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content. Turn on Speak Selection and Speak Screen, then swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen to hear page content read aloud.
- **Official link:** https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/hear-the-screen-spoken-aloud-iphone

#### Android Select to Speak
- **Cost:** Free
- **Platforms:** Android
- **Built in:** Yes — built into Android at no extra cost
- **Setup:** Open Settings > Accessibility > Select to Speak and turn it on. Tap the Play button or tap items on screen to hear them read aloud.
- **Official link:** https://support.google.com/android/answer/9206284

### Reading & writing — Reading aid (dyslexia-friendly font)

#### OpenDyslexic
- **Cost:** Free (open source font)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux, Web
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Download the font from opendyslexic.org and install it on your system, or add a browser extension that renders pages in OpenDyslexic.
- **Official link:** https://opendyslexic.org/

### Motor & dexterity — Voice transcription / dictation

#### OpenAI Whisper (local)
- **Cost:** Free (open source; runs locally on your own computer)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, Linux
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Install Whisper (`pip install openai-whisper`) or use a desktop front end such as Whisper.cpp. Drop in an audio file and get a local transcript with no data leaving your computer.
- **Official link:** https://github.com/openai/whisper

### Focus & energy — ADHD / low-energy task helper

#### Goblin.tools
- **Cost:** Free (donations welcome)
- **Platforms:** Web (any device)
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Open goblin.tools in any browser. Use Magic To-Do to break big jobs into steps, Formalizer to rewrite text, Estimator for time, and Compiler to turn brain dumps into task lists. No account required.
- **Official link:** https://goblin.tools/

### Focus & energy — ADHD / low-energy habit helper

#### Habitica
- **Cost:** Free (free tier; optional paid subscription)
- **Platforms:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Create a free account at habitica.com, add habits, dailies, and to-dos, and check them off to earn in-game rewards. Works well for low-energy days because small wins still count.
- **Official link:** https://habitica.com/

### Focus & energy — ADHD / low-energy focus timer

#### Pomofocus
- **Cost:** Free (free web timer; optional account)
- **Platforms:** Web (any device)
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Open pomofocus.io, set your work and break intervals, and start the timer. A simple Pomodoro loop helps when starting is the hardest part.
- **Official link:** https://pomofocus.io/

### Focus & energy — ADHD / low-energy task list

#### Microsoft To Do
- **Cost:** Free (free with a Microsoft account)
- **Platforms:** Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Web
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Sign in with a free Microsoft account, create a list (try 'Today'), and add tasks. Use 'My Day' to pick a few small tasks each morning so lists stay manageable.
- **Official link:** https://to-do.microsoft.com/

### Focus & energy — ADHD / low-energy visual organizer

#### Trello
- **Cost:** Free (free tier with unlimited cards and up to 10 boards per workspace)
- **Platforms:** Web, iOS, Android
- **Built in:** No
- **Setup:** Create a free account at trello.com, make a board, add lists (To Do / Doing / Done), and drop cards on them. Drag-and-drop boards work well for visual thinkers.
- **Official link:** https://trello.com/
